Our friend lives on an unadopted road their house is 200yds up, the drive is 200yds and they are rural but not off grid and are surrounded by woodland. Most couriers will not drive up the unadopted road. They have oil central heating. It's generally tricky to get deliveries because of lorry access.
You need a septic tank, solar panels, LPG or oil for heating (oil boilers are being phased out). The condition of the 1.5 mile, unadopted road will impact on the septic tank being emptied and delivery of gas/oil. They may refuse. Presume couriers will not deliver. Presume refuse/recycling is roadside so 1.5 miles from the front door.
There is access to the house from the neighbour property, that seems to be part of the issue (they seem to not want people living at the property so are causing nuisance). Bats cannot be removed or interfered with so they are staying put. Presumably as there is no mains water, it's a bore hole and the council will send someone round to test the water quality, annually. There's filtration etc. to ensure it's safe to use. It's not suitable for anyone immunocompromised, might not be safe for children.
It failed to reach reserve at auction and the risk with auction, once the hammer falls, you own it. So if you want to spend £2,500 on a survey before auction, that may confirm issues with the building. Presume at least £500k for costs to make it liveable. You are unlikely to get a mortgage until the bank are satisfied about the liveability of the property and unlikely to extend a mortgage to cover all the costs. You need a good solicitor that can sort out the land issues as it's complex with boundaries, access etc. It's not, in my opinion, a prospect for a family. It could be well over a year before it's ready for habitation. Then aside of that as long as you don't want anything delivered like white goods, furniture, groceries, courier deliveries. It should be fine.
